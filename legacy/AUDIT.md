# Audit of the first-submission pipeline

The files in `legacy/` are the code and aggregate results of the first submission, kept for
transparency. They are **superseded** by `dpleak/` and `results/`. Re-examining them during the
second revision surfaced the following problems; all results in the revised manuscript come
from a complete re-run with the corrected pipeline.

## 1. Canary evaluated against the wrong profile (24 of 252 published rank cells)

`legacy/utils.py:extract_canary_labels()` read the canary profile from
`data/{dataset}/{dataset}_input_sample.json` **by list position** (index 0 = Tagset A).
A profile that was never inserted in any corpus was later prepended to the i2b2 file, and the
i2b2 10-document Tagset A models were evaluated after that change. Separately, the MEDDOCAN
100-document Tagset A models (non-DP and ε = 8) were scored with the e-mail address of the other
profile. In those cells the "true" candidate (line `0:` of `candidates_<tuple>.txt`) does not
contain the inserted values:

| Condition | Budgets | Tuples affected |
|---|---|---|
| i2b2, Tagset A, 10 documents | non-DP, ε = 8, ε = 4 | N, P, E, A, (N,A), (N,A,E) — 18 cells |
| MEDDOCAN, Tagset A, 100 documents | non-DP, ε = 8 | E, (N,A,E), (N,A,P,E) — 6 cells |

`python legacy/audit_canary_eval.py --results <legacy results/output>` reproduces the list
(`legacy/canary_eval_audit.json`). The i2b2 cells were the basis of the statements that, on
i2b2, some identifiers became *more* memorised under DP; with the inserted profile they do not.
Fix: `dpleak/spec.py` pins every profile by value, exactly as written in `canaries/*.xml`, and
`results/legacy_rescore.json` re-scores all published adapters with the pinned values.

## 2. Synthetic corpus sizes (reviewer A.2c)

`legacy/evaluation.py` appended to `filtered_labels.csv` on every re-run, so the stored files held
2–3 copies of the same 1,000 generations. Unique-item counts are unaffected, but per-document rates
computed from those files were wrong. Fix: `dpleak/generate.py` writes exactly 1,000 non-empty
documents once; `dpleak/leakage.py` reads `generations.csv` directly.

## 3. Substring matching

Training values were matched as substrings, so one- and two-character i2b2 annotations (`F`, `Mo`,
`03`) matched inside unrelated words. `dpleak/leakage.py` reports substring and whole-word counts.

## 4. Hand-assembled tables (reviewer B.1, B.2)

The 100-document canary table had been assembled by hand with the dataset labels swapped and was
incomplete. All tables are now generated by `scripts/make_tables.py` from `results/*.json`.

## 5. Smaller issues

- Candidate alternatives were drawn without a seed; they are now the full pool of 10,000 values
  (single identifiers) or seeded random combinations (tuples, three draws).
- Notes were read in `os.listdir` order (file-system dependent) before the train/held-out split;
  they are now sorted.
- The DP accountant composed `epochs * (1/q + 1)` steps instead of the steps actually run.
- Runs used 2-GPU DDP (batch 16 per GPU); they now use one GPU with gradient accumulation (same
  effective batch of 32), which makes the sampling order depend only on the training seed.
